Common questions

What is Veystead?

Veystead is job management software for UK trade businesses. It connects jobs, quotes, invoices, employees, timesheets and job profitability in one app.

Can I use Veystead on my phone?

Yes. The web app works on modern phone and desktop browsers, and an iOS version is being prepared for the App Store.

How much does an employee cost?

The owner is included in the £19 monthly price. Each additional active employee or user costs £7.99 per month.

Can I cancel the subscription?

Yes. The owner can cancel from Plan & Billing. Access continues until the end of the current trial or paid period, and the next automatic renewal is stopped.

Is Veystead only for electricians?

No. It is designed for electricians, plumbers, heating engineers, builders, carpenters, roofers, decorators, landscapers and other small field-service teams.
