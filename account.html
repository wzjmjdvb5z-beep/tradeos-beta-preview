Open TradeOS
IN THE APP
More → Account & privacy

From there you can see the signed-in account, open the privacy notice, terms and support pages, and submit an account-deletion request.

Account deletion

Sign in to TradeOS, open More, choose Account & privacy, then select Request account deletion. You must type DELETE to confirm. The request is queued for completion within seven days, subject to legal retention requirements and any workspace-ownership steps that need resolving.

Cannot sign in?

If you cannot access the in-app deletion control, email TradeOS_UK@proton.me from the email address associated with the account. Do not send your password or authentication codes.
