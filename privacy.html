Information we process

Depending on how TradeOS is used, we may process account email and authentication identifiers; names, roles and company memberships; company profile and billing-plan information; customer names and contact details; job titles, addresses, schedules and notes; photos or files a user chooses to attach to job notes; quotes and quote responses; timesheets, timers and approval information; invoices, payment records, material and expense information; business bank details entered for invoices; support messages, beta feedback, audit/security events and account-deletion requests.

Why we use it

We use personal information to authenticate users, provide shared company workspaces, run quote-to-job, scheduling, timesheet, invoice and payment-recording workflows, calculate business metrics, provide customer document links, prevent misuse, investigate faults, provide support, process account-deletion requests and meet legal obligations.

Lawful bases

Where UK data-protection law applies, the relevant basis will depend on the processing. We expect to rely on contract or steps before entering a contract to provide the service; legitimate interests for proportionate security, support and product improvement; legal obligation where required by law; and consent where a feature specifically requires it.

Trade-business customer data

A trade business using TradeOS may decide why customer, employee or contact information is entered and used. In those cases the trade business will often act as controller and TradeOS may act as a processor/service provider for that information. Businesses must have an appropriate basis for the personal information they enter and should avoid storing information that is not needed for the work.

Authentication, local storage and tracking

The web app uses Supabase Auth. Signed-in browser sessions are persisted using browser storage so users can remain signed in and refresh their session. TradeOS does not currently use advertising trackers or sell personal information to advertisers. The hosting/CDN and backend providers may receive ordinary technical information such as IP address, device/browser information, timestamps and request logs as part of delivering and securing the service.

Service providers

The current web launch uses Supabase for authentication, database and file-storage services; GitHub Pages for static web hosting; jsDelivr to deliver the pinned Supabase JavaScript client; and Proton Mail for the listed support mailbox. Where mobile beta or App Store distribution is used, Apple and build/distribution tooling such as Expo/EAS may also process the information required to provide that distribution. Providers may process information in different countries and appropriate transfer safeguards are used where required by applicable law.

Customer quote and invoice links

A business can create an expiring tokenised link to a quote or invoice. Anyone with a valid unexpired link may be able to view that specific document; quote links may also allow the recipient to accept or decline and select optional extras. Treat these links as confidential. They do not provide access to the rest of the business workspace.

Security

The service uses Supabase authentication, row-level security on application tables, company-scoped access policies, role checks for manager actions, least-privilege database grants, restricted public RPC access, private file storage for job-note uploads and expiring tokenised customer-document links. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention

Workspace and account information is generally kept while the service is used and until deletion is requested, subject to legitimate legal, security, backup and dispute-resolution needs. Customer/business records may need to be retained by the business using TradeOS for its own legal or accounting obligations. Support, feedback and audit/security information is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Account deletion

Signed-in users can open More → Account & privacy and submit an account-deletion request in the app. Requests are targeted for completion within seven days. If the account owns a workspace, ownership transfer or workspace deletion may need to be resolved first. Information that must lawfully be retained may be kept for the required period.

Your rights and complaints

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase or restrict personal information, object to certain processing and receive certain information in a portable format. Contact TradeOS_UK@proton.me to make a request. You may also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.
