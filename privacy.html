TRADEOS PRIVACY

Privacy notice

How TradeOS handles personal information during the founding beta.

SupportBack to preview
WHO WE ARE
TradeOS

TradeOS is the trading name used to operate this job-management service for trade businesses. For privacy questions, rights requests or data-protection complaints, contact TradeOS_UK@proton.me.

Contact TradeOS

Information we process

Depending on how the service is used, TradeOS may process account email and authentication identifiers; team names, roles and company memberships; workspace information; job titles, addresses, schedules and notes; timesheet and approval information; customer or contact information entered by a trade business; job values, labour costs, materials, expenses and profitability information; beta feedback, support messages and account-deletion requests.

The current native beta does not intentionally collect advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, camera data or microphone data.

Why we use personal information

We use personal information to create and secure accounts, provide company workspaces and team access, run job and timesheet workflows, calculate job profitability, provide password recovery, investigate bugs and abuse, respond to support and beta feedback, process deletion requests and meet legal obligations where applicable.

Our lawful bases

Where UK data-protection law applies, TradeOS expects to rely on contract or steps before a contract to provide the service; legitimate interests to secure, support and improve the service where those interests do not override individual rights; legal obligation where the law requires processing; and consent only where a feature specifically requires it.

Business customer data

A trade business may enter information about customers, staff or contacts into TradeOS. In those workflows, the trade business will often decide why that information is used and may be the data controller, while TradeOS provides the service and processes the information on its behalf where applicable. Businesses should only enter information they are authorised to use.

Service providers and hosting

TradeOS currently uses Supabase for authentication, database and backend services; Expo / EAS for mobile build and distribution tooling where applicable; and Apple for TestFlight and App Store distribution. The current Supabase production project is hosted in the EU West (Ireland) region.

Some service providers may process information outside the UK. Where restricted international transfers apply, TradeOS will use or rely on appropriate safeguards required by applicable data-protection law. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

How long we keep information

Account and workspace information is generally kept while the account or business uses TradeOS and until it is deleted, subject to legal, security and operational requirements. Support, beta feedback and security records are kept only as long as reasonably needed for support, product improvement, security and accountability. During the founding beta, account-deletion requests are targeted for completion within seven days, subject to lawful retention requirements and any workspace-ownership steps that must be resolved.

Account deletion

Signed-in users can initiate account deletion from the Account & Privacy section of the TradeOS app. During the founding beta, requests are queued for operator completion within seven days. Account and associated personal information will be removed unless TradeOS is required to retain specific information by law or for a necessary security or dispute purpose. Owner accounts may require transfer or deletion of the related workspace as part of the process.

Security

TradeOS uses native secure session storage, database row-level security, tenant-scoped authorisation, least-privilege client credentials, privileged server-side database functions for sensitive workflows, automated database/security tests and release secret scanning. No system can guarantee absolute security, and these controls are reviewed as the product develops.

Your rights and complaints

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase or restrict personal information, object to certain processing and receive certain information in a portable format. Where processing relies on consent, consent can be withdrawn without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Send privacy requests or data-protection complaints to TradeOS_UK@proton.me. You may also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ICO data-protection complaint guidance.

Automated decisions and tracking

The current TradeOS beta does not use personal information for advertising tracking and does not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects solely through automated processing. Job profitability is a business calculation based on workspace values and approved costs; it is not an eligibility or profiling decision about an individual.

Changes to this notice

TradeOS will update this notice when its data practices or legal obligations materially change. The date at the top of this page shows the current version.

This notice describes the founding beta as currently built. Before a wider paid public launch, TradeOS will review the final business identity, production subprocessors, retention arrangements and customer data-processing terms against the final service.
